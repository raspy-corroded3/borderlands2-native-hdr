## bl2hdr 1.0.1-beta.1

HDR settings in the game's own menu, a separate cutscene brightness, and a clean exit instead of a frozen
game after a graphics-driver crash.

### New
- **HDR settings page: Options > Video > HDR** (the row below Window Mode). It has:
  - **HDR** Off / On
  - **Game brightness**, **HUD and menu brightness**, **Cutscene brightness** (80–500 nits)
  - **Peak brightness** (400–2000 nits)
  - **Highlight strength** (0–200 %)

  Changes apply immediately and are saved to `bl2hdr.ini`. Works with keyboard, controller and mouse.
- **Cutscene brightness**, new setting `[hdr] VideoWhiteNits`. It defaults to the HUD brightness, so
  nothing changes until you set it.
- **Graphics driver crash (TDR):** 9on12 cannot recover a lost GPU device, and the game used to freeze.
  bl2hdr now shows a message and closes the game. New setting `[d3d9] OnDeviceRemoved`: `message`
  (default), `exit` (close without a message) or `continue` (old behaviour).

### Fixes
- **Some cutscenes were brighter than in the original game** (e.g. the character trailers). The game's
  video shader doesn't clamp its colours. The original 8-bit output did that by itself, bl2hdr's 16-bit
  output didn't. Cutscenes now look like the original, at your cutscene brightness.
- **Frame captures** (a developer feature) failed after some resolution changes.

### Checked, no change needed
- Cel-shading outlines: unaffected by HDR.
- ECHO log videos (the hologram): look exactly like the original.

### Upgrading
Replace `d3d9.dll`. Your `bl2hdr.ini` keeps working: the new keys use their defaults when missing. The
HDR settings page writes the brightness values into it for you. To see all options with comments, compare
with the new `bl2hdr.ini`.

### Known issue: "ran out of video memory" after long sessions
Unchanged from 1.0.0-beta.2. BL2 is a 32-bit game, and 9on12 uses about 300 MiB more of its 4 GiB
address space than plain D3D9. If the game stops with this message, set `PoolSize=256` under
`[TextureStreaming]` in `Documents\My Games\Borderlands 2\WillowGame\Config\WillowEngine.ini`.

### Testing
Tested on Windows 11 with an NVIDIA RTX 3070 Ti: automated in-game runs (cutscenes and ECHO logs played
and compared with the original output, simulated GPU device removal, menu navigation, resolution
changes), no errors, frame rate unchanged. The menu was also checked by hand with mouse and controller.
**Not yet tested on AMD/Intel GPUs or laptops with two GPUs.** Reports are very welcome.
